This website posts a logic riddle/math problem every day.
There's an AI-powered hint and verification system but the problems are allegedly picked by a Human.
Regardless, I've looked at a couple of problems already and enjoyed solving them.
Here's an example of “today's” problem:

 > In a city there are 100 people and several clubs.
 > Each club has an odd number of members.
 > In addition, any two distinct clubs always share an even number of members.
 > What is the maximum possible number of clubs?
